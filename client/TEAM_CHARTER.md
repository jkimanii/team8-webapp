One paragraph description: The app is a Strathmore-centred online marketplace/e-commerce site where users (mainly Strathmore students) can list their items and sell and buy from each other


**Resources**

|Resource|What it represents|
|------|------|
|Users|Students who buy and sell; name, email, password, campus|
|Listings|Items/services for sale; title, price, condition, description, image, category, seller|
|Categories|Textbooks, Electronics, Fashion, Furniture, Services, Food|



**Actions per resource**

Users
* Register
* Log in

Listings
* Create a listing
* View all listings
* View a single listing
* Filter listings by category
* Search listings by title

Categories
* View all categories
